This whole blog can be generated with a prompt under a second but yet I feel like its more important than ever to write about things using a human brain now & share it with the world so I'm starting a blog.

## First post

A lot of software engineering is almost dead and its fine. They were writing the same code over and over again anyways.

A lot of jobs like HRs, accountants, lawyers, etc will be gone too, but why are people so attached to them? Those jobs have only been here for a couple years & its not like anyone's life purpose is to balance the books.

What's next? Nobody has a clue, only guesses.
