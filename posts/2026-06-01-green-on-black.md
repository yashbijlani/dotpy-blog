A blog is a small thing. It should look small.

Green text on a black background is not a design system, it is a mood. It says: this was typed, not assembled.

## Rules I kept

1. one font
2. one colour
3. nothing that moves

No cards. No hero. No newsletter modal. If a page needs a spinner, the page is wrong.

```python
def main():
    print("hello from the terminal")
```

The whole stylesheet is a few hundred bytes and you can read it in one sitting. That is the entire feature.
